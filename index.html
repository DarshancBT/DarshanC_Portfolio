InvisiLock - An Emergency Aware Smart Door Lock System

Developed “InvisiLock,” an emergency-aware smart door lock system designed to improve safety, accessibility, and emergency response during critical situations such as fire accidents, medical emergencies, or electronic sabotage attempts. Unlike conventional smart locks with exposed external electronics, InvisiLock features a concealed internal locking mechanism to prevent tampering and unauthorized access. The system supports multiple unlocking methods including a dedicated mobile application, proximity-based sensing mode, and a secure handle-based PIN entry system for situations where a mobile device is unavailable.

An additional remote emergency communication module continuously monitors emergency alerts and can autonomously trigger safety actions such as automatic unlocking, distress signal transmission, emergency contact notifications, and secure PIN sharing with trusted contacts. The system is also capable of using nearby connected devices for automatic calling and distress messaging when the user is trapped or unable to respond. The project combines embedded systems, IoT communication, emergency automation, and smart security design to create a safer and more reliable next-generation smart locking solution.

MediWatch - A Patient Health Monitoring and IV Control Device

Developed “MediWatch,” a smart patient monitoring and IV management system designed to remotely track patient health parameters and improve hospital caregiving efficiency. The system continuously monitors vital signs such as heart rate, SpO2, body temperature, room temperature, humidity, and IV drip status, and transmits the data in real time to the MediWatch mobile application. The prototype also includes an automatic IV safety mechanism that detects empty drip conditions and autonomously closes the valve to prevent reverse blood flow.

The mobile application logs all received medical data, generates detailed health reports, and provides convenient remote monitoring support for doctors, caretakers, and hospital staff. The project integrates embedded systems, IoT communication, healthcare automation, sensor fusion, and mobile application development to create a safer and more intelligent patient monitoring solution.

Smart Solar Electric Fence - An Autonomous Solar Based Shock Intensity Controller Prototype

Developed a smart solar-powered electric fence safety system designed to reduce the risk of fatal electric shocks in conventional fence protection systems. The prototype uses an Arduino Uno, MOSFET-based switching, custom high-voltage sensing circuitry, boost converters, BMS modules, and solar-powered battery management to autonomously control shock intensity levels. The system cycles through multiple shock modes ranging from mild to high intensity by controlling the charging duration of the high-voltage circuit. A custom copper-coil sensing mechanism was developed to detect high-voltage DC discharge activity, enabling safer and more intelligent fence operation. The project focuses on controlled energy delivery, renewable power integration, and improved human and animal safety in electric fence applications.
